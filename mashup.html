Before you use the mashup builder

The builder works with some personal data. This is exactly what, and where it goes:

  • Your Codeforces handle: read from the Codeforces page header when you are logged in, or typed by you.
  • Your submissions: fetched from the public Codeforces API to leave out problems you have solved and to score your contests.
  • Your mashup activity: the contests you generate, their results and share codes, and your form choices.

All of it is stored only in this browser. The only network requests go to codeforces.com/api. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else, there are no analytics or ads, and you can delete everything from this page at any time. Privacy policy
